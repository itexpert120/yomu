# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who read EPUB books on Android phones, tablets, foldables, and larger windows. They use Yomu for focused, long-form reading, library organization, reference lookups, and tracking their reading without depending on a network connection.

## Product Purpose

Yomu is a polished, offline-first EPUB reader built for comfortable daily reading and deliberate library management. It succeeds when the reading canvas stays primary, advanced controls remain approachable, and the experience feels purpose-built across phone and tablet layouts.

## Brand Personality

Calm, reader-first, and crafted. Yomu should feel like a serious premium reading tool: quiet during reading, confident in its information density, and distinctly native without resembling a stock Android template.

## Anti-references

- Default Material app shells, controls, cards, and purple theme conventions.
- Reader interfaces whose chrome competes with the book.
- Phone layouts stretched across tablets without structural adaptation.
- Decorative blur, glass, or animation that reduces legibility or interrupts reading.
- Feature-heavy settings presented as one undifferentiated control dump.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the reading canvas first and let chrome recede when it is not needed.
2. Make advanced reading controls deep but coherent, with consistent custom primitives.
3. Treat tablets and larger windows as intentional layouts, not enlarged phones.
4. Preserve a restrained, recognizable Yomu identity across light, dark, and OLED modes.
5. Use motion to explain state and structure while keeping text visually stable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support scalable type, strong contrast, reduced motion, large touch targets, screen-reader labels, and states that do not rely on color alone. Keep light and dark themes equally legible, respect RTL where the reading engine allows it, and prioritize long-session visual comfort.
